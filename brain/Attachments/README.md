# Attachments

Small supporting files may live here when they materially help the brain notes.

Preferred: Markdown, text, JSON, YAML, CSV, SVG, small PNG/JPG/WebP, and small PDFs.

Keep files under 2 MB. Large media, invoice archives, exports, backups, databases, installers, and raw customer data belong elsewhere.

Never store credentials, secrets, customer PII, or private keys here.
